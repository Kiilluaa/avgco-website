# Third-Party Data

The weekly crossword publishing script retrieves its candidate answer list from the MsFit Crossword Dataset by Nicole Feng:

https://github.com/nzfeng/crossword-dataset

The dataset is distributed under the GNU General Public License v3.0. Its license is available in the source repository:

https://github.com/nzfeng/crossword-dataset/blob/main/LICENSE

Definitions used to form clues are retrieved through the Datamuse API:

https://www.datamuse.com/api/
